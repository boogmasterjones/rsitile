What It Costs

Costs vary by project: a kitchen backsplash typically runs $2,000–$5,000, a bathroom remodel $7,000–$15,000, and whole-home floor tile $10,000–$20,000 or more. We'll give you an exact, written quote based on your specific project during a free in-home estimate — not a number over the phone.

What to Expect, Start to Finish

  1. Call or request an estimate online. Tell us a bit about the project so we can prepare for the visit.
  2. Free in-home estimate. We measure the space, discuss material options, and answer your questions in person.
  3. Written quote. You get a clear, firm scope of work and price before deciding anything.
