What Makes a Good Tile Contractor

Licensing, insurance, a real in-home estimate, and a written scope of work are the baseline — not extras. Beyond that, experience with the specific type of project matters: waterproofing know-how for a shower, slope and drainage planning for a lanai, layout precision for a kitchen backsplash at eye level. FAQ

Tile Contractor FAQ

What does a tile contractor do?

A tile contractor handles the full scope of a tile installation project — demolition of old flooring or tile, substrate prep and waterproofing, layout planning, setting the tile, and grouting and sealing the finished surface. That's different from just knowing how to set tile; the prep work before and finishing work after are what determine whether the job lasts.

How much does it cost to hire a tile contractor in Southwest Florida?

Costs vary widely by project: a kitchen backsplash typically runs $2,000–$5,000, a bathroom remodel $7,000–$15,000, and whole-house floor tile $10,000–$20,000 or more. We'll give you an exact, written quote based on your specific project during a free in-home estimate.

What's the difference between a tile contractor and a general contractor?

A general contractor coordinates an entire remodel across multiple trades — framing, electrical, plumbing, tile, and more — often subcontracting the tile work out. A tile contractor specializes specifically in tile installation and waterproofing. For a tile-only project, hiring a tile contractor directly usually means more specialized expertise and one less layer between you and the crew actually doing the work.

How do I find a reliable tile contractor near me in Southwest Florida?

Look for a licensed, insured contractor with real local reviews and a track record in your specific city, then get a free in-home estimate with a written quote before committing.
