        <h2>Bradenton Neighborhoods We Serve</h2>
        <p>Homes in Bradenton are not all the same. A home in the historic downtown needs one plan. A new build near Lakewood Ranch needs another. Here's how that plays out in the areas we work in most:</p>
        <h3>Downtown Bradenton &amp; the Riverwalk</h3>
        <p>Look at older homes near downtown and the Manatee River Riverwalk. They often still have their first tile or grout. It can be decades old. The floors under it may be uneven. The tile may not match anything sold today. So careful matching matters more here than in new homes. So does planning where old tile meets new. Leveling the base matters more too.</p>
        <h3>Palma Sola &amp; West Bradenton</h3>
        <p>Homes near Palma Sola Bay get damp, salty air. It is the same air we see on the coast in Sarasota and Venice. Porcelain and grout that fights stains are often the better pick here. They beat plain ceramic.</p>
        <h3>Cortez</h3>
        <p>The historic fishing village of Cortez sits right on the water. It has old cottages and newer homes rebuilt on the water. So we often match old tile in one home. A few doors down, we put in all-new, damp-proof tile.</p>
        <h3>Lakewood Ranch Area</h3>
        <p>East Bradenton keeps growing toward Lakewood Ranch. That keeps us busy with whole-home tile in new homes. We tile kitchens, floors, and bathrooms as part of a new build. It is not a remodel.</p>
        <h3>Near Anna Maria Island</h3>
        <p>Some Bradenton homes on the mainland sit close to the Anna Maria Island causeway. They get some of the same coast air as the island. So here too, the tile you pick matters more than it would inland.</p>
