          <h2>Why Is Sealing Out Water the Key to a Good Shower?</h2>
          <p>A shower gets wet more than any other spot in your home. So the seal behind the tile matters as much as the tile. We have built custom showers in Southwest Florida for more than 30 years. Each job starts with a true seal layer behind the tile. Then we slope the base so water runs to the drain. A coat of sealer over cement board is not enough. We have torn out and rebuilt lots of bathrooms that others got wrong. We build ours so they don't make the same mistakes.</p>
          <p>We do simple tub-to-shower swaps. We also build fully custom curbless showers. Those can have a long, thin drain, a wall shelf, and a seat. Our own crew does the framing, sealing, layout, setting, and grout. You get a clear, written list of the work before we start.</p>
          <h2>How We Build Your Shower</h2>
          <ul>
            <li><strong>Free in-home estimate.</strong> We measure and talk about the layout. Curb or curbless? Where does the shelf go? Then you get a firm, written quote.</li>
            <li><strong>Tear-out and framing.</strong> We take out the old shower or tub walls. We change the framing if you want seats or shelves.</li>
            <li><strong>Seal and slope.</strong> We put in the seal layer. We slope the floor to the drain. Curbless showers can get a long, thin drain.</li>
            <li><strong>Layout and setting.</strong> We plan the tile so the cuts match on each side. We set it with the right thin-set for that tile and spot.</li>
            <li><strong>Grout, seal, and walk-through.</strong> We grout and seal the tile. We look over the shower with you. Then we call the job done.</li>
          </ul>
