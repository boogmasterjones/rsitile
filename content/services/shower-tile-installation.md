          <h2>Why Is Waterproofing the Most Important Part of a Shower Remodel?</h2>
          <p>Because a shower takes on more sustained water exposure than any other surface in your home, so the waterproofing behind the tile matters as much as the tile itself. Rock Solid Tile has built custom showers across Southwest Florida for more than 30 years, and every job starts with a proper waterproofing membrane and correctly sloped pre-pitch to the drain — not just a coat of sealant over cement board. We've torn out and rebuilt countless bathrooms that other contractors got wrong, and we build ours to avoid the same mistakes.</p>
          <p>From a simple tub-to-shower conversion to a fully custom curbless shower with a linear drain, niche, and bench, our crew handles framing, waterproofing, layout, setting, and grouting in-house, with a clear written scope before we start.</p>
          <h2>Our Shower Tile Installation Process</h2>
          <ul>
            <li><strong>Free in-home estimate</strong> — we measure, discuss layout options (curb vs. curbless, niche placement), and provide a firm, written quote.</li>
            <li><strong>Demo &amp; framing</strong> — removal of the old shower or tub surround, with any framing changes needed for benches or niches.</li>
            <li><strong>Waterproofing &amp; pre-slope</strong> — membrane installation and correct slope to the drain, including linear drain systems for curbless showers.</li>
            <li><strong>Layout &amp; setting</strong> — tile is planned for symmetrical cuts and set with the right thin-set for the material and location.</li>
            <li><strong>Grouting, sealing &amp; walkthrough</strong> — grout and sealant are applied, and we review the finished shower with you before calling the job complete.</li>
          </ul>
          <p>Want more detail before you start? Read our <a href="/blog/shower-tile-installation-guide">complete shower tile installation guide</a> for a full cost breakdown, material comparisons, and common mistakes to avoid.</p>
