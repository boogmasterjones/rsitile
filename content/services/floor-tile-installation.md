          <h2>Why Does a Florida Slab Need Extra Prep?</h2>
          <p>Most homes in Southwest Florida sit on a concrete slab. So a tile floor is as much about the prep as the finished look. We have put in floor tile here for more than 30 years. We do one-room updates. We also do whole-home floors that flow from room to room with no breaks. They run right through open living spaces.</p>
          <p>Our crew tears out the old floor. We level the base. We plan the layout for big tile and patterns. Then we set, grout, and seal. We add gaps called expansion joints. They let the floor move as Florida's heat and damp air change. Whole-house floor tile often costs $10,000–$20,000. Bigger whole-home jobs that add kitchen and bathroom tile often run $20,000–$50,000.</p>
          <h2>How We Tile Your Floors</h2>
          <ul>
            <li><strong>Free in-home estimate.</strong> We measure your space and talk about tile choices. You get a firm, written quote.</li>
            <li><strong>Tear-out and prep.</strong> We take out the old floor. We pour a self-leveling base where needed so it is flat.</li>
            <li><strong>Layout plan.</strong> Before we cut, we plan the pattern. We plan where rooms meet and where the joints go.</li>
            <li><strong>Setting.</strong> We set the tile with the right thin-set and trowel for its size.</li>
            <li><strong>Grout, seal, and walk-through.</strong> We grout and seal the floor. Then we look it over with you.</li>
