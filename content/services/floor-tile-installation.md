    answer: "Porcelain is a true fired tile — extremely durable and permanent, but harder underfoot. LVP (luxury vinyl plank) is a resilient plank or tile product that's softer, warmer, and often faster to install, but not as long-lived as porcelain.           <h2>Why Does Floor Tile Installation Need Special Prep on a Florida Slab?</h2>
          <p>Because most homes in Southwest Florida are built on a concrete slab, floor tile installation is as much about substrate prep as it is about the finished look. Rock Solid Tile has installed floor tile across the region for more than 30 years, from single-room updates to whole-home flooring that runs seamlessly through open-concept living spaces.</p>
          <p>We handle demolition of old flooring, subfloor leveling, layout planning for large-format tile and pattern work, setting, grouting, and sealing — with proper expansion joints planned in for Florida's heat and humidity swings. Whole-house floor tile installation typically costs $10,000–$20,000, and larger whole-home projects that also include kitchen and bathroom tile typically run $20,000–$50,000.</p>
          <h2>Our Floor Tile Installation Process</h2>
          <ul>
            <li><strong>Free in-home estimate</strong> — we measure your space, discuss material options, and provide a firm, written quote.</li>
            <li><strong>Demo &amp; subfloor prep</strong> — removal of old flooring and self-leveling underlayment where needed for a flat surface.</li>
            <li><strong>Layout &amp; expansion planning</strong> — we plan tile pattern, room transitions, and expansion joints before cutting.</li>
            <li><strong>Setting</strong> — tile is set with the correct thin-set and trowel size for the material and format.</li>
            <li><strong>Grouting, sealing &amp; walkthrough</strong> — grout is applied and sealed, and we review the finished floor with you.</li>
          </ul>
