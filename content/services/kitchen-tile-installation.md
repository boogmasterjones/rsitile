          <p>In a kitchen, small mistakes show. Grout lines have to line up with the cabinets and outlets. Floor tile has to run clean under the toe-kicks. It has to fit neatly around islands. We have tiled kitchens in Southwest Florida for more than 30 years. We plan the layout with the same care on each job. It might be a small backsplash update. Or it might be a full kitchen remodel.</p>
          <p>Our own crew does it all. We prep the base. We plan the layout around cabinets and big kitchen machines. We set, grout, and seal. You get a clear, written list of the work before we start.</p>

          <ul>
            <li><strong>Free in-home estimate.</strong> We measure your kitchen. We talk about backsplash and floor choices. You get a firm, written quote.</li>
