          <h2>What Makes Kitchen Tile Installation Different From Other Rooms?</h2>
          <p>Kitchen tile is one of the least forgiving places to get details wrong — grout lines have to line up with cabinets and outlets, and floor tile has to run cleanly under toe-kicks and around islands. Rock Solid Tile has installed kitchen tile across Southwest Florida for more than 30 years, with the same attention to layout whether it's a small backsplash refresh or a full kitchen remodel.</p>
          <p>We handle substrate prep, layout planning around cabinets and appliances, setting, grouting, and sealing in-house, with a clear written scope of work before we start.</p>
          <h2>Our Kitchen Tile Installation Process</h2>
          <ul>
            <li><strong>Free in-home estimate</strong> — we measure your kitchen, discuss backsplash and/or floor options, and provide a firm, written quote.</li>
            <li><strong>Layout planning</strong> — we plan tile placement around cabinets, outlets, appliances, and islands before any cutting starts.</li>
            <li><strong>Substrate prep</strong> — self-leveling underlayment or backer board installed as needed for a flat, stable surface.</li>
            <li><strong>Setting</strong> — backsplash and/or floor tile is cut and set to the planned layout.</li>
            <li><strong>Grouting, sealing &amp; walkthrough</strong> — stain-resistant grout is applied and sealed, and we review the finished kitchen with you.</li>
          </ul>
