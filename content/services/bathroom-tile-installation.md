 <h2>Why Is Bathroom Tile Different in Florida?</h2>
 <p>A bathroom gets wet each day. Few rooms in your home get wetter. So the base under the tile matters more here than on any other job. It must be sealed. The floor must slope to the drain. We have tiled bathrooms in Southwest Florida for more than 30 years. We work from Port Charlotte and Punta Gorda to Venice and Sarasota. We build each job around getting that prep right. How the tile looks is not the only thing. Our own crew does the tear-out, sealing, setting, and grout.</p>
 <p>You may want to redo one tub wall. Or you may want to strip the bathroom down to the studs. Either way, our own crew does it all. We tear out, put up backer board, and seal. We lay out, cut, set, and grout. Each estimate comes with a clear, written list of the work. So you know just what to expect before we start.</p>
 <h2>How We Tile Your Bathroom</h2>
 <ul>
 <li><strong>Free in-home estimate.</strong> We measure and talk about tile choices. You get a firm, written quote.</li>
 <li><strong>Tear-out and prep.</strong> We take out the old tile. Then we put in the right backer board or a seal layer.</li>
 <li><strong>Layout and dry fit.</strong> We plan the cuts and lines first. So the tile is centered and even. We don't just start in a corner.</li>
 <li><strong>Setting and grout.</strong> We set the tile with the right thin-set and trowel for that tile. Then we grout and seal it.</li>
 <li><strong>Final walk-through.</strong> We look over the bathroom with you. Then we call the job done.</li>
 </ul>
