## What Makes a Good Tile Contractor

Licensing, insurance, a real in-home estimate, and a written scope of work are the baseline — not extras. Beyond that, experience with the specific type of project matters: waterproofing know-how for a shower, slope and drainage planning for a lanai, layout precision for a kitchen backsplash at eye level.
