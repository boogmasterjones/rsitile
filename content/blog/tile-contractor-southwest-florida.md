## What Makes a Good Tile Contractor

The basics are a license and insurance. A real in-home estimate and a written list of the work are basics too. They are not extras. Past that, skill with your kind of job matters. A shower needs know-how about sealing out water. A lanai needs a plan for slope and drainage. A kitchen backsplash sits at eye level, so the layout must be exact.
