If you're planning a bathroom remodel in Port Charlotte, Punta Gorda, North Port or Venice, the first question is usually the same: what is this going to cost? The honest answer is that it depends on your bathroom. But you can still get a realistic starting point and understand what pushes a project up or down.

This guide explains the typical ranges Rock Solid Tile publishes for tiled bathroom work, what changes the number, and why a written quote tells you far more than a phone ballpark.

## Typical Cost Ranges for a Tiled Bathroom Remodel

Rock Solid Tile publishes these typical ranges for homeowners:

- **Bathroom remodel:** about $7,000–$15,000
- **Custom shower:** about $4,000–$10,000

These are typical ranges, not prices. The final number depends on the details below. Treat them as a planning tool so you can set a budget before the estimate.

## What Moves the Price

### Size of the Bathroom

More square footage means more tile, more setting materials and more labor. A compact hall bath with a tub surround is a different job from a primary bathroom with a large walk-in shower and a full tiled floor.

### Tile Material

Tile choice is one of the biggest swings in any budget. The type of tile you pick affects both the material cost and the amount of labor involved.

If you're matching tile in an older home, sourcing a close match can take extra work. Rock Solid Tile offers tile matching in older homes and tile supply and sourcing, which can help when the original tile is no longer easy to find.

### Demolition and Subfloor Leveling

What's underneath matters as much as what goes on top. Removing old flooring takes time, and the condition of the floor underneath is not always known until the old material comes up. Uneven subfloors need leveling before new tile goes down. Old flooring removal and subfloor leveling are part of what Rock Solid Tile handles, and demolition is done in-house.

### Waterproofing

Waterproofing is part of the cost of a tiled shower. Rock Solid Tile does its waterproofing in-house.

### Niches and Benches

Each niche or bench adds to the work involved, so the more custom features you add, the higher the price tends to go. Custom showers with niches and benches are among the services Rock Solid Tile offers.

### Tub-to-Shower Conversion

Some homeowners swap a tub for a walk-in shower. Both tub-to-shower conversions and curbless showers are part of Rock Solid Tile's work. For more on what goes into a tiled shower, see our [shower tile installation guide](/blog/shower-tile-installation-guide).

## Why a Written Quote Beats a Phone Ballpark

It's tempting to call around and ask for a number over the phone. The problem is that nobody can see your subfloor or measure your walls from a phone call. Demolition, leveling, waterproofing and the specific tile you pick all change the cost.

A written estimate helps by covering things like:

- Talking through your tile choices and features like niches, benches or a curbless entry
- Putting everything in writing so you know what's included

Rock Solid Tile offers free estimates with written quotes. A written quote gives you something concrete to compare and plan around.
