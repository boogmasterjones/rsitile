## Typical Cost Ranges for a Tiled Bathroom Remodel

We post these typical ranges for people planning a bathroom:

- **Bathroom remodel:** about $7,000–$15,000
- **Custom shower:** about $4,000–$10,000

These are typical ranges, not prices. The final number depends on the things below. Use them as a planning tool. That way you can set a budget before the estimate.

## What Moves the Price

### Size of the Bathroom

More square feet means more tile. It means more setting supplies and more work hours. A small hall bath with tile around the tub is one kind of job. A main bathroom is a very different job. It may have a big walk-in shower and a full tile floor.

### Tile Type

The tile you pick is one of the biggest swings in any budget. It changes the cost of the tile. It also changes how much work the job takes.

Are you matching tile in an older home? Then finding a close match can take extra work. We match tile in older homes. We also help you find and buy tile. That helps when the old tile is hard to find now.

### Tear-Out and Leveling the Floor

What is under the tile matters as much as what goes on top. Taking out an old floor takes time. And you can't always know the shape of the floor under it. You find out when the old floor comes up. An uneven floor needs leveling before new tile goes down. We take out old floors and level the base. Our own crew does the tear-out.

### Sealing Out Water

Sealing out water is part of the cost of a tiled shower. Our own crew does the sealing.

### Wall Shelves and Seats

Each wall shelf or seat adds to the work. So the more custom touches you add, the higher the price tends to go. We build custom showers with wall shelves and seats.

### Tub-to-Shower Conversion

Some people swap a tub for a walk-in shower. We do tub-to-shower swaps. We build curbless showers too. Want more on what goes into a tiled shower? See our [shower tile installation guide](/blog/shower-tile-installation-guide).

## Why a Written Quote Beats a Guess Over the Phone

It's tempting to call around and ask for a number over the phone. The problem is that no one can see your floor over the phone. No one can measure your walls from a call. The tear-out, leveling, sealing, and the tile you pick all change the cost.

A written estimate helps. It covers things like:

- Talking through your tile choices and extras like shelves, seats or a curbless entry
- Putting it all in writing so you know what is included

We give free estimates with written quotes. A written quote gives you something real to compare and plan around.
