This Privacy Policy describes how Rock Solid Tile ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use our website, call us, text us or request an estimate.

## Information we collect

When you contact us we may collect your name, phone number, email address, zip code, the address of the job and the details you give us about your project. Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site; that data is aggregated and does not identify you.

## How we use it

We use your information to answer your request, give you an estimate, schedule and complete the work, and follow up afterwards. We do not sell your personal information.

## Text messages

If you give us your mobile number and check the consent box on our form, or reply YES to a text from us, we may send you customer care text messages: a callback confirmation, follow-ups on a request you made, appointment reminders, and one review request after a completed job. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out and HELP for help. Consent to texts is not a condition of purchase.

**We will not share your mobile phone number or text message consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.** Our messages are sent through a service provider that handles delivery only.

## Sharing

We share information only with the service providers who help us run the business (for example scheduling, website hosting and text message delivery), and only for that purpose. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

## Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the details we hold about you, and you can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP.
