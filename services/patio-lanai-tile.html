Outdoor Tile Built for Florida Weather

A lanai or pool deck has to handle direct sun, heavy seasonal rain, and constant wet feet, so outdoor tile installation calls for different materials and prep than an indoor floor. Rock Solid Tile has installed patio and lanai tile across Southwest Florida for more than 30 years, with slip resistance and proper drainage built into every layout.

We handle substrate and slope evaluation, layout and expansion joint planning, setting with outdoor-rated thin-set, and weather-resistant grouting and sealing — whether it's a screened lanai, an open patio, or tile around an existing pool deck.

Our Patio & Lanai Tile Installation Process

  • Free in-home estimate — we measure your outdoor space, discuss slip-resistant material options, and provide a firm, written quote.
  • Slab & slope evaluation — we check existing drainage and slope away from the home before planning layout.
  • Layout & expansion planning — pattern and expansion joints are planned for Florida's heat and seasonal rain.
  • Setting — tile is set with outdoor-rated thin-set suited to sun and moisture exposure.
  • Grouting, sealing & walkthrough — weather-resistant grout and sealant are applied, and we review the finished space with you.

FAQ

Patio & Lanai Tile Questions

Is your patio and lanai tile slip-resistant?

Yes. Outdoor and pool-deck areas call for textured, slip-resistant tile rated for wet-foot traffic, and we help you choose a material and finish that meets that standard without sacrificing the look you want.

Can you tile around an existing pool deck?

Yes, we regularly install tile around existing pools and spas, including coping and deck areas, working carefully around the pool shell and any equipment.

Do you tile screened lanais as well as open patios?

Yes, both. Screened lanais and open patios are prepped and installed a little differently — a screened lanai stays drier day to day, while an open patio needs full outdoor-rated materials and drainage planning — and we'll spec the right approach for your space.

How do you handle drainage and expansion joints outdoors?

Outdoor tile needs a proper slope away from the home and expansion joints placed to handle Florida's heat swings and seasonal rain. We plan both as part of every patio and lanai layout, not as an afterthought.
