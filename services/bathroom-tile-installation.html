Bathroom Remodels Built to Last in Florida's Climate

A bathroom sees more daily moisture than almost any other room in your home, which makes correct tile installation critical. Rock Solid Tile has installed bathroom tile across Charlotte and Sarasota Counties — from Port Charlotte and Punta Gorda to Venice and Sarasota — for more than 30 years, and we build every job around proper waterproofing, slope, and substrate prep — not just how the finished tile looks.

Whether you're updating a single tub surround or gutting a bathroom down to the studs, our crew handles demolition, cement board or waterproof membrane installation, layout, cutting, setting, and grouting in-house. Every estimate includes a clear, written scope of work, so you know exactly what to expect before we start.

Our Bathroom Tile Installation Process

  • Free in-home estimate — we measure, discuss material options, and provide a firm, written quote.
  • Demo & substrate prep — removal of old tile and installation of proper backer board or waterproofing membrane.
  • Layout & dry fit — we plan cuts and pattern lines so tile is centered and symmetrical, not started in a corner.
  • Setting & grouting — tile is set with the correct thin-set and trowel size for the material, then grouted and sealed.
  • Final walkthrough — we review the finished bathroom with you before calling the job complete.

Materials

Bathroom Tile Materials We Install

We'll help you choose the right material for your bathroom's moisture level, budget, and style.

Porcelain Tile

Dense, low-absorption, and available in large formats — our most common recommendation for bathroom floors and tub surrounds.

Ceramic Tile

A budget-friendly option in a wide range of colors and sizes, well suited to walls and lighter-traffic bathroom floors.

Natural Stone

Marble, travertine, and slate bring a premium, one-of-a-kind look and require professional sealing for bathroom use.

Mosaic & Glass Tile

Ideal for shower floors, niches, and accent bands where texture, grip, or a decorative detail is needed.

FAQ

Bathroom Tile Installation Questions

How long does a bathroom tile installation take?

Most single-bathroom tile installations take 3–7 working days depending on scope — a tub surround replacement is faster than a full floor-to-ceiling remodel with a custom shower. We'll give you a specific timeline during your free estimate.

Do you handle waterproofing, or just the tile?

We handle the entire installation, including cement board, waterproofing membranes, and proper slope to the drain. Correct waterproofing is what prevents leaks and tile failure down the road, so we never skip this step.

Can you match or replace tile in an existing bathroom?

Yes. If you're only replacing a section of tile, we'll do our best to match existing tile or grout color, or advise on a clean transition point if an exact match isn't available.

Do I need to buy the tile myself, or can you provide it?

Either way works. We can source and supply tile for you through our suppliers, or install tile you've already purchased yourself — we'll go over material options and pricing during your free estimate so you can decide what makes sense.

Do you offer free estimates for bathroom tile projects?

Yes, every bathroom tile estimate is free and comes with a written quote. Call (941) 276-0305 or request an estimate online to get started.
