Flooring Built for Florida Slabs and Open Floor Plans

Most homes in Charlotte and Sarasota Counties are built on a concrete slab, which means floor tile installation is as much about substrate prep as it is about the finished look. Rock Solid Tile has installed floor tile across the region for more than 30 years, from single-room updates to whole-home flooring that runs seamlessly through open-concept living spaces.

We handle demolition of old flooring, subfloor leveling, layout planning for large-format tile and pattern work, setting, grouting, and sealing — with proper expansion joints planned in for Florida's heat and humidity swings.

Our Floor Tile Installation Process

  • Free in-home estimate — we measure your space, discuss material options, and provide a firm, written quote.
  • Demo & subfloor prep — removal of old flooring and self-leveling underlayment where needed for a flat surface.
  • Layout & expansion planning — we plan tile pattern, room transitions, and expansion joints before cutting.
  • Setting — tile is set with the correct thin-set and trowel size for the material and format.
  • Grouting, sealing & walkthrough — grout is applied and sealed, and we review the finished floor with you.

FAQ

Floor Tile Installation Questions

What's the difference between porcelain tile and LVT flooring?

Porcelain is a true fired tile — extremely durable and permanent, but harder underfoot. LVT (luxury vinyl tile) is a resilient plank or tile product that's softer, warmer, and often faster to install, but not as long-lived as porcelain. We'll help you weigh the tradeoffs for your space and budget.

Can you install large-format tile in a Florida slab home?

Yes. Large-format tile needs a flat, well-prepped slab and the correct trowel and thin-set for the tile size, which we account for during layout. We also plan expansion joints appropriately for Florida's heat and humidity swings.

Do you remove old flooring before installing new tile?

Yes, demolition and removal of existing carpet, vinyl, or old tile is included in our floor tile estimates, along with any subfloor leveling needed before new tile goes down.

How long does whole-home floor tile installation take?

A single room typically takes 2–4 working days, while a whole-home floor tile project can take 1–3 weeks depending on square footage, pattern complexity, and how much furniture needs to move. We'll give you a specific timeline during your free estimate.
