The Kitchen Update That Ties Everything Together

A new backsplash or floor is one of the fastest ways to change how a kitchen feels, but it's also one of the least forgiving places to get details wrong — grout lines have to line up with cabinets and outlets, and floor tile has to run cleanly under toe-kicks and around islands. Rock Solid Tile has installed kitchen tile across Southwest Florida for more than 30 years, with the same attention to layout whether it's a small backsplash refresh or a full kitchen remodel.

We handle substrate prep, layout planning around cabinets and appliances, setting, grouting, and sealing in-house, with a clear written scope of work before we start.

Our Kitchen Tile Installation Process

  • Free in-home estimate — we measure your kitchen, discuss backsplash and/or floor options, and provide a firm, written quote.
  • Layout planning — we plan tile placement around cabinets, outlets, appliances, and islands before any cutting starts.
  • Substrate prep — self-leveling underlayment or backer board installed as needed for a flat, stable surface.
  • Setting — backsplash and/or floor tile is cut and set to the planned layout.
  • Grouting, sealing & walkthrough — stain-resistant grout is applied and sealed, and we review the finished kitchen with you.
FAQ

Kitchen Tile & Backsplash Questions

Can you tile just my backsplash, or floors too?

Either one. Many customers start with just a backsplash update, while others want the kitchen floor and backsplash done together for a matched look. We'll scope whichever you need during your free estimate.

How long does a kitchen backsplash installation take?

Most backsplash-only projects take 1–3 working days. A full kitchen floor and backsplash job typically takes 4–7 working days depending on square footage and tile pattern.

Do you tile around outlets, under cabinets, and around appliances?

Yes. Backsplash work involves careful cutting around outlets, switches, and cabinet edges, and floor tile is set to run cleanly under toe-kicks and around islands and appliances. This detail work is part of every kitchen tile estimate.

What grout should I use in a kitchen?

We typically recommend a stain-resistant grout for both backsplashes and floors, since kitchens see frequent spills and grease. Sealing isn't always necessary depending on the grout type — we'll go over grout color and whether sealing makes sense for your kitchen during the estimate.

Yes, every kitchen tile and backsplash estimate is free and comes with a written quote. Call (941) 276-0305 or request an estimate online to get started.
