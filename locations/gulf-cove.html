Tile Installation for Gulf Cove's Boating Lifestyle

Gulf Cove is built around the water — canals and riverfront lots along the Myakka River, many with private docks and seawalls. That waterfront lifestyle means lanais and pool decks get used as much as any room in the house, so Rock Solid Tile pays close attention to slip resistance and moisture-tolerant materials on every Gulf Cove project.

From indoor bathroom and kitchen remodels to outdoor lanai and pool deck tile, we handle substrate prep, layout, setting, and grouting in-house, with a clear written scope of work before we start.
