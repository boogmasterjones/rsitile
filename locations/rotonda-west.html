Tile Installation for a Planned Golf & Canal Community

Rotonda West is laid out around several golf courses and a network of canals, with deed restrictions that keep the community's look consistent from street to street. Rock Solid Tile has installed tile throughout Rotonda West for years, and we're comfortable coordinating with HOA guidelines on lanai and exterior work in addition to standard interior remodels.

Lanai and pool deck tile is especially popular here, since so many homes back up to a fairway or canal view worth spending time looking at. We handle substrate prep, layout, setting, and grouting in-house on every project, indoors or out.
